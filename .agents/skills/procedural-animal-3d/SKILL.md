# Procedural Vertebrate Animals & Birds (`procedural-animal-3d`)

Follow the [organic fauna policy](../procedural-kitbash-3d/references/organic-fauna.md) and the anatomical proportions in [vertebrate anatomy](./references/vertebrate-anatomy.md).

## Non-Negotiable Anti-Slop Guardrails

- **Zero geometric primitives, uniform capsules, or single-tube revolution trunks (`VERTEBRATE_TORSO_SILHOUETTE`):** never represent a trunk as a single scaled ellipsoid, uniform capsule/sausage tube, single lofted revolution mesh, or identical 5-station hourglass peanut across guilds. Compose mammal torsos (`buildMammalCore` in `greebles/organic/vertebrates.ts`) from an axial `sculptedLoft` trunk plus integrated 3D anatomical muscle volumes (`role: 1`, `hullPrimary` with world-space `makeMammalBodyColor` bounds so coat markings flow across volumes without foreign color patches): paired **scapular shoulder masses**, paired **gluteal/iliac haunch masses**, and a **pectoral sternum/brisket mass** tailored to each species' body plan, with a **>= 1.28x interior height/width silhouette ratio**. Differentiate guild silhouettes: slab-sided high-withered antelopes/cervids without artificial top-view hourglass waist pinches, low-front/high-rear coiled springing felines and lagomorphs, front-massive scruff-mantled lupines vs slender upright bat-eared desert canids, dome-humped camelids, rounded-hump plantigrade bears, torpedo-wedged tapirs, and flat-backed barrel cavies.
- **Seamless muscular haunches, shoulders & crisp calcaneal hocks (`VERTEBRATE_LIMB_ANATOMY`):** never use stick legs, C-banana lower hind legs, or bolted-on lateral drumstick shelves. Anchor upper limb roots (`scapTop`/`scapMid` and `hipTop`/`hipMid` in `vertebrateLimbs.ts`) medially inside the overhanging 3D scapular shoulder and gluteal haunch masses so limbs emerge downward with zero horizontal step or drumstick shelf. Sculpt upper limbs (`role 4`) with deep sagittal muscle wedges (`hind thigh` sagittal depth **>= 1.65x** lower limb width, `fore upper arm` sagittal depth **>= 1.45x** lower limb width), caudal muscle-belly bias, and root-damped upward normals (`sculptedLimbSegment`). Shape lower hindlimbs (`role 5`) with a 5-point anatomical `stifle -> gaskin calf -> calcaneal hock heel -> straight cannon -> pastern` curve and >= 6 mm ball-in-socket joint overlap.
- **Differentiated cranial archetypes (`skullProfile`), binocular vs panoramic eyes & sculpted ruffs:** never extrude neck, cranium, and muzzle as a single monotone cone or attach warty `organ` balls as neck manes. Assign each mammal a `skullProfile` (`canid`, `feline`, `ungulate`, `camelid`, `ursine`, `rodent`, `lagomorph`, `tapir`, `prosimian`) with species-authentic eye placement (forward binocular eyes `0.58..0.64 * hw` on predators/primates vs lateral panoramic eyes `0.78..0.92 * hw` on herbivores) and continuous `sculptedLoft` neck manes, scruff capes, dewlaps, nape crests, and cheek ruffs.
- **Rich sub-part count & tight triangle budget:** every vertebrate model must assemble **>= 22 sculpted sub-parts** while staying strictly under **3,500 triangles**.
- **Recessed brow-shaded eyes (`<= 25%` cranium width):** always tuck `glassVisor` eyes under sculpted brow ridges (`eye <= 0.25 * craniumWidth`), never oversized protruding marble eyes.
- **No HDR bloom blowouts (`channel <= 218`):** keep albedos natural (`max RGB channel <= 218`, e.g., capped whites `0xc4c8d0`, warm ochres `<= 0xc8a482`) so directional sunlight and `UnrealBloomPass` never wash pale coats or plumage into glowing white blobs.
- **Differentiated bird wings & fanned tails:** use `sculptedWingFoil` for separate cambered inner secondary (`role 7`), upper coverts, and outer hand wing (`role 8`), tipped with individual shingled `sculptedFeather` primary blades (`>= 6` outer parts) and `>= 4` individually fanned tail rectrices (`role 9`).
- **Tissues & startup textures:** stamp coat parts with `tissue = 6` (`warmFurPelage` fine directional guard-hair PBR bundle) and feathered parts with `tissue = 7` (`avianFeatherVane` barbule/rachis PBR bundle); keep horns, beaks, claws, and hooves `tissue = 4`, eyes `tissue = 5`, `metalness = 0`.
- **Mandatory workflow:** run `npm run validate:models -- --category fauna --strict` + `npm run test:models` + inspect `iso`, `front`, `side`, `top` and poses in `/playground.html?inspect=fauna.<id>&pose=...` across all three biomes.
