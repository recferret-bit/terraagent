# Organic fauna policy

Owner-approved 2026-11-05 for insect creatures and species hives. These exceptions override mechanical appearance prescriptions in kitbash, animation, texture, shader and validator skills only for fauna; robot/building rules otherwise remain unchanged.

- Forms may use custom smooth lofts, swept tapered appendages, sculpted shell patches, irregular mounds and hollow organic entrances. Broad curved surfaces need no mounted greebles. Geometry carries silhouette; procedural texture/shader fields carry fine pores, growth lines, veins and scars.
- Use anatomical surface profiles: nonmetallic chitin, flexible membrane, glossy eyes, secretory sacs, soil and dry shell. No mandatory 60/30/10 ratio, gunmetal joints, distinct color per limb group, accent stripe or glow. Maintain species and pose readability under actual lighting and biome weathering.
- Allow anatomy-aligned UVs and procedural color/roughness/normal fields on merged kitbash fauna. Patterns share a coherent source field. Filter fine frequencies at distance. Cache generated maps; never allocate textures per creature or per frame.
- Keep shell segments rigid. Explicit flexible channels may deform membranes/sacs within a documented strain envelope, while anchors stay attached. Mirror every GPU motion in validator poses and shadow passes. Do not disable collision, attachment, ground or triangle checks.
- Use a fauna appearance profile with positive anatomical/material checks instead of mechanical plate/color checks. Add known-good and known-bad fixtures for new checks. Smoothness and lack of glow are not defects; disconnection, invalid materials and robot-like morphology still require attention.
- Keep the existing catalog, unified material, deterministic cached generation, instancing compatibility and <3.5k production triangles. Any higher detail experiment requires a measured budget and production fallback, not an arbitrary global budget increase.
- Review all four views in three biomes, idle/walk/attack, emission-off and gameplay scale. Take artistic risks in shape and surface, then use visual comparison to select the result. Numeric validation does not judge anatomy or taste.

## Current implementation

- `frontend/render/kitbash/greebles/organic/shapes.ts`: `organ`, closed tapered `tendril`, recessed `burrow`; authored smooth normals and anatomy-aligned UVs survive merging. `aOrganic`: 0 mechanical/default, 1 chitin, 2 membrane, 3 secretory tissue, 4 soil/keratin, 5 eye/rhinarium, 6 mammalian fur pelage (`warmFurPelage` PBR bundle), 7 avian feather plumage.
- `organic/legs.ts`: reusable six-leg rig. Organic `6/16` uses explicit alternating tripods; mechanical spider phases are unchanged.
- `frontend/render/kitbash/greebles/organic/vertebrates.ts`, `vertebrateLoft.ts`, `vertebrateLimbs.ts`, `avianBuilder.ts`: sculpted vertebrate anatomy builders (`sculptedLoft`, `sculptedLimbSegment`, `sculptedWingFoil`, `sculptedFeather`, `sculptedEar`, `buildMammalCore`, `buildAvianCore`, `cervineAntlers`, `sweptHorns`) powering the 12 biome-endemic warm-blooded mammals (`archetypes/units/warmFauna.ts` + `warmFaunaSpecs.ts`) and 15 biome-endemic birds (`archetypes/units/avianFauna.ts` + `avianFaunaSpecs.ts`). See `procedural-animal-3d` for modeling rules.
- `frontend/render/animation/vertebrates/`: semantic vertebrate vertex animation module (`aVertJoint`, `aVertTip`, `pose.ts`, `shader.ts`, `instanceShader.ts`, `validationPoses.ts`) solving closed-form 2-link quadruped IK (`role 4/5/6`), avian inner/outer wing flap/fold (`role 7/8`), tail/head stabilization (`role 2/3/9/10`), and per-instance `aBioState` streaming with 100% CPU/GPU/shadow parity. See `procedural-animal-animation`.
- `frontend/render/visuals/terrain/treeBreeds.ts` & `groundFloraGeos.ts` + `frontend/render/planets/hex/flora/forestPipeline.ts`: 11 biome-endemic 3D tree breeds (`alpinePine`, `silverBirch`, `ancientOak`, `weepingWillow`, `coconutPalm`, `stiltMangrove`, `canopyBanyan`, `coralBlossom`, `oasisDatePalm`, `desertDrySnag`, `joshuaAcacia`), 4-stage ecological forest stand pipeline (`generateCandidateTreePlaces` $\rightarrow$ `evaluateForestStand` $\rightarrow$ `stratifyStandSites` $\rightarrow$ `synthesizeTreePhenotype`, shared across `HexTerrain` via `generateTileForestTrees` and `PlanetSurface` via `applySurfaceForestPipeline`), and 3D ground flora (`grassTuft`, `wildflower`, `bush`, `saguaro`, `barrelCactus`, `reeds`).
- `frontend/render/visuals/unified/organicSurface.ts`: shared filtered growth/vein/grain/pelage/plumage fields modulate pigmentation, roughness and micro-normal response (`uFurPelageMap` / `uFurRoughnessMap` from neutral `warmFurPelage` for `tissue = 6`; `uFeatherVaneMap` / `uFeatherRoughnessMap` from `avianFeatherVane` for `tissue = 7`). Derivatives execute outside tissue-dependent branches. Surface fields and shared startup textures never allocate per creature or per frame.
- Insect channels `49` (jaw hinge), `59` (antenna probing), `69` (1.8% gland strain riding body 9), and `60` (1.8% static hive gland strain) serve arthropods (`frontend/render/animation/insects/`); vertebrate mammals (`tissue 6`) and birds (`tissue 7`) use `VERTEBRATE_ANIMATION` (`aVertJoint`/`aVertTip`). All run through shared visible/depth shaders and CPU validator poses, and support instanced biosphere rendering via `aBioState`.
- Organic appearance checks: `ORGANIC_TISSUE`, `ORGANIC_METAL`, `ORGANIC_ROUGHNESS`, `ORGANIC_FLEX`, `ORGANIC_TOO_DARK`, `ORGANIC_UNIFORM`, plus vertebrate anti-slop checks `VERTEBRATE_DETAIL_RICHNESS` (`>= 22` sub-parts), `VERTEBRATE_RIG_PARITY`, `VERTEBRATE_TORSO_SILHOUETTE` (`>= 1.28x` interior torso height/width contrast + integrated 3D scapular shoulder, gluteal haunch, and pectoral brisket muscle volumes, rejecting uniform capsules and single-tube revolution trunks), `VERTEBRATE_LIMB_ANATOMY` (joint continuity + `>= 1.65x` sagittal hind haunch taper), `VERTEBRATE_WING_ARTICULATION`, `VERTEBRATE_EYE_PROPORTION` (`<= 25%` cranium width), and `VERTEBRATE_BLOOM_SAFE` (`maxCh <= 218`). Good/bad fixtures in `validate/organicFixtures.ts` run in both Vitest and the skill's `self-test.mjs`.
- Current sacs are opaque PBR with thickness-inspired pigmentation, not physical transmission or subsurface scattering.
