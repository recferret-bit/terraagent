# TerraAgent — Whitepaper

> Outward-facing specification updated 2026-11-24. Matches [`README.md`](https://github.com/recferret-bit/terraagent/blob/main/README.md), `@terra/sim` deterministic rules, and the live NEAR Testnet deployments + 7-crate Rust workspace. Fee rates, staking splits, and guild parameters are configurable protocol defaults calibrated in the 7-day economy simulator (`/dev/economy-sim/`). *Note: this document describes game and protocol mechanics and makes no promise regarding any token value.*

## 1. Thesis: the economy is the gameplay

Traditional strategy games stop when you close the browser tab and patch balance on spreadsheets. Early blockchain games inverted the problem: tokens came first and gameplay came second. TerraAgent builds a real-time colony RTS where supply, terrain scarcity, trade routes, diplomacy, and autonomous AI directors form one continuous economy across NEAR Protocol and hardware-attested NEAR.AI.

## 2. Web3 ownership & first-class NEAR accounts

- **Human Pilots:** Drop in immediately via FastAuth, OAuth, or 10 NEAR wallets (Meteor, MyNearWallet, Intear, OKX, NEAR Mobile, Bitte AI, HOT, HERE, Nightly, Sender) to provision `{pilot}.pilots.terraagent.testnet`. Authenticated `NEP-366` meta-transactions (`POST /v1/near/relay`) share a global daily gas budget, while atomic `claimAccount` rotates `[AddKey, DeleteKey]` to full self-custody and optionally seals a revocable scoped trading session key so 1-click orderbook trading (or direct `walletSignature` signing) works without surrendering custody.
- **Colony Sub-Accounts & Social Identity:** Isolated sub-accounts hold `NEP-245` orbital cargo (`mt_batch_transfer`, `mt_transfer_call`, holder `mt_withdraw`, NEP-145 storage bounds), lock native payable NEAR and cargo escrows (`open_near_escrow`, `claim_expired_refund`), vote in Planet Council DAOs (with SputnikDAO v2 JSON export), and share soulbound `NEP-171` Colony Deeds (`prune_history`, NEP-181 pagination) directly to **NEAR Social**.
- **AI Directors, Hosted MCP & Shade MPC:** Autonomous Directors use HMAC-keyed deterministic Ed25519 keys (`deriveDirectorPublicKey`) bound via finite-gas on-chain `AddKey(FunctionCall)`/`DeleteKey`. External agents trade through the **Hosted MCP Server** (`POST /v1/mcp`, `GET /v1/mcp/agent-market.json`, `POST /v1/trade/agent/rfq`) alongside decentralized **Shade Agents** + **Chain Signatures MPC (`v1.signer`)**.
- **Guild Traders & Protocol Treasury:** NPC freighters (`kestrel`, `vanguard`) quote VWAP-anchored spreads paired with a live **1Click Cross-Chain Deposit/Withdraw Ramp** on `/trade.html`, while `protocol.terraagent.testnet` collects organic protocol shares to fund gasless onboarding and Managed NEAR.AI.

What autonomous agents **cannot** do without a human: bypass runtime governance (kill switches, paused policies, revoked director keys, live exposure limits, or VWAP price bands), inject instructions across isolated staker/player `<untrusted_player_label>` fences, exceed ACID spend caps, break an active peace truce or cancel a war, lock human NEAR in bonds, dismantle buildings, claim ownership keys, or stake validator NEAR (`403 AI_STAKING_FORBIDDEN`).

## 3. Player income, lossless staking, 4-way settlement & TerraAgent Protocol

- **Cargo Exports (`100%` to Seller):** Mine surface commodities and wrap them 1-to-1 into `18`-decimal `NEP-245` cargo tokens on `res.terraagent.testnet` across all `10` T1–T3 resources — **Iron** (`metals.t1`), **Light Alloys** (`metals.t2`), **Titanium** (`metals.t3`), **Silica** (`minerals.t1`), **Rare Earths** (`minerals.t2`), **Exotic Crystal** (`minerals.t3`), **Water** (`bio.water`), **Biomass** (`bio.biomass`), **Petroleum** (`bio.t2`), and **Gene-stock** (`bio.t3`) — then sell for USDC, NEAR, or barter cargo on `/trade.html`.
- **Trade Hub Stall Royalties (`0–1.00%`):** Every built Trade Hub acts as a commercial storefront earning `0–100 bp` maker royalties on filled orders.
- **Scoped Trading Bots & Bounties:** Automated `ta_bot_...` bots arbitrage orbital spreads within ACID daily limits, while mercenary defense bounties and breached peace/war bonds pay `90%` directly to victims or defenders.
- **4-Way Atomic Settlement:** Every trade clears atomically via counter-signed `NEP-413` `token_diff` intents across four recipients on top of notional: Seller Principal (`100%`), Stall Owner (`0–1.00%`), Planet Council DAO (`20%` of platform fee = `0.20%` standard / `0.13%–0.05%` Tier 1–4 staker), and `protocol.terraagent.testnet` (`80%` of platform fee = `0.80%` standard / `0.52%–0.20%` Tier 1–4 staker; when a planet has a seated Tier 4 Lead Patron, `15%` of Protocol's cut = `12%` of gross settles to the Patron and `68%` stays with Protocol). On a `$100.00 USDC` fill with a `0.50%` stall fee, a standard buyer pays `$101.50` (`$101.15` at Tier 1, `$100.75` at Tier 4): `$100.00` to Seller, `$0.50` to Stall Owner, `$0.20` (`$0.13`–`$0.05`) to Planet DAO, and `$0.80` (`$0.52`–`$0.20`) to Protocol + Lead Patron.
- **Wallet-First 4-Tier Orbital Patronage & Multi-Colony Slots (W28):** Any connected human NEAR wallet stakes on `staking.terraagent.testnet` with no Colony Deed required (native `NEAR` in 1 tx via `stake_payable`, or whitelisted `stNEAR` / `LiNEAR` via `ft_transfer_call`; AI callers and `ta_bot_...` keys receive `403 AI_STAKING_FORBIDDEN`). Pilots keep `100%` principal plus `80%` of validator yield (`7.20%` net APY from `9.00%` simulated gross APY on mock/testnet; live validator APY on mainnet), while `20%` (`1.80%` APY slice) funds `TerraAgent Protocol`. Zero combat or harvesting buffs: `@terra/sim` never reads staking (`D-31`).

- **Four Staker Profit & Effective APY Buckets (`/me.html#staking`, Self-Fills Excluded):**
  1. **Net Validator Yield (`80%`):** Live-accruing `7.20%` simulated net APY on testnet/mock (or live validator APY on mainnet) across native `NEAR`, `stNEAR`, and `LiNEAR`, with `0%`-fee `compound_yield`.
  2. **Market Taker Fee Savings:** Direct buyer savings from `1.00%` down to `0.65%` (instant at `10 NEAR`) and `0.50% / 0.35% / 0.25%` (`50 / 200 / 500+ NEAR` after `24h` warmup).
  3. **Trade Hub Stall Royalties (`0–1.00%`):** Passive maker royalties on local orderbook fills at your Trade Hub. The `100 bp` cap is intentionally identical across all tiers (`D-31`) to keep `@terra/sim` decoupled from staking and prevent negative-margin wash arbitrage.
  4. **Tier 4 Lead Planetary Patron Revenue (`15%` of Protocol Cut):** The top active `≥ 500 NEAR` staker backing one of the 3 canonical public worlds (`Kharon Dunes`, `Thalassa Verde`, `Granite Hollow`; paid sovereign NFT worlds excluded) automatically holds its Lead Patron seat, receiving `15%` of Protocol's trade-fee cut (`12%` of gross on standard `20%` DAO fills) plus an orbital `ISS <pilot>.near` Flagship Carrier. Self-fills where buyer and seller/stall owner share a wallet are excluded from Effective APY and Patron revenue.
- **Anti-Snipe, Warmup, Compound, Unbonding & AI Budget Rules:**
  - **Fee Warmup & Dust Pre-Stake Reset:** Tier 1 (`65 bp`) activates immediately (`t = 0`). Deeper Tier 2–4 discounts (`50 / 35 / 25 bp`) require `24h` of continuous stake (`1 min` in `NEAR_MODE=local`). Crossing from `< 50 NEAR` into `>= 50 NEAR` resets the effective `stakedSinceMs` warmup clock across both single-pool and cross-pool positions, closing dust pre-stake bypasses while preserving the clock on `>= 50 NEAR` top-ups.
  - **Early Exit, Compound & Unbonding:** Unstaking principal `< 7 days` after the latest deposit (`< 1 min` in local mode) pays `0.25%` (`25 bp`) to `protocol.<root>`; matured stakes (`>= 7 days`) exit at `0%`. `compound_yield` carries `0%` fee and never resets either the `24h` warmup clock or the `7-day` maturity clock. Unbonding takes `4 epochs` (~`2 game-days`; `1 min` in local mode); unbonding principal is excluded from active tier calculations immediately upon unstake (instant downgrade if remaining active stake drops below a tier threshold).
  - **Live Managed AI Budget (`>= 65%` Protocol Margin):** Wallet-wide daily USD cap = `35% × (daily protocol yield USD + daily non-self protocol trade-fee USD)` at live `NEAR/USD`, where trade fees sum today's `FINAL` buyer fills after deducting Planet DAO (`20%`) and Lead Patron (`15%` of protocol cut) shares and excluding self-fills. At `$0.0000375` per batched `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` turn, this buys `~18 / ~90 / ~350 / ~900` daily calls across Tiers 1–4 at `$4/NEAR` before trade-fee bonus calls. Multi-colony expansion (3 / 6 / 12 colonies at T2–T4) spans planets or distinct sectors (`409 SECTOR_ALREADY_COLONIZED` blocks two colonies in the same sector).
  - **On-Chain Solvency & Custody Guards:** `staking` tracks total native backing (`TOTAL_NATIVE_BACKING_KEY`) so operator withdrawals can never touch user native deposits or yield reserve, supports optional `op_id` idempotency on `compound_yield` and `withdraw_lst` (with Option A price-mode LST yield payout), and `AccountClaimService` blocks cross-wallet re-claims (`403 NEAR_NOT_OWNER`).
- **Bonded Diplomacy & 6-Hour War Escrows:** Bonded truces (`2h–72h`) and `6h` War Bonds (granting `2x` Raider carry capacity `40 -> 80`) lock collateral on `bounty.terraagent.testnet`. Peaceful truce completion or meeting war objectives (`>= 1` razed structure or `>= 200` siphoned resources) refunds `100%`; early betrayal or failed sieges slash `90%` (`18.0 NEAR` on a `20 NEAR` bond) to the counterparty and `10%` (`2.0 NEAR`) to Protocol.
- **Why `TerraAgent Protocol` Scales Net-Positive (Zero Token Printing):** Three organic inflows (`0.80% / 0.52%–0.20%` trade fee share across standard and Tier 1–4 stakers, `1.80%` validator yield slice, and `10%` bond slashes) fund the two real Web3/AI operating costs: `relayer.terraagent.testnet` gasless `NEP-366` meta-transactions (`~0.0003 NEAR/tx` within a `5 NEAR/day` cap and `<= 0.05 NEAR` attached deposit limit, plus `0.02 NEAR` cold-root pilot provisioning — at an illustrative `~$4/NEAR`, one `$100 USDC` fill's `+$0.80 ≈ 0.20 NEAR` funds `~650` player meta-txs) and batched `NEAR AI Cloud` TEE turns (`z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` sweeps packing Rival bots into 5-colony batches with a `30 s–180 s` tier-scaled state-digest burst cooldown ≈ `$0.03–$0.05/planet/day`, while per-wallet Managed AI budgets dynamically cap at `35%` of protocol income for a `>= 65%` margin). Just `~$7/day` of planet trade volume (`$7 × 0.80% = $0.056/day`) or `200 NEAR` of planet staked TVL (`4 × 50 NEAR` pilots generating `+3.60 NEAR/yr ≈ $14.40/yr` or `~$0.04/day` at `~$4/NEAR`) covers an entire persistent 24/7 planet; all rates are live-inspectable at [`GET /v1/near/protocol`](/v1/near/protocol).

## 4. Verifiable NEAR.AI commanders, Agent Commerce, 25-Agent Swarms & Guild pricing

- **Orbital Guild VWAP Pricing:** Scheduled NPC freighters (`kestrel`, `vanguard`) quote every T1 pair from trailing `24h` organic VWAP (min `3` eligible fills) via `mid = ref * clamp((T + 10) / (I + 10), 0.50, 2.00)^0.5`, widening half-spreads from `2%` (`4%` full spread) to `15%` on the thin inventory side while exporting `10%` of surplus off-planet on departure.
- **Governed TEE Commanders, In-Browser Proofs & Encrypted BYOK:** Rival AI colonies and Managed Staker Autopilot run batched `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` turns exclusively when protocol-managed on **NEAR AI Cloud** (`cloud-api.near.ai/v1`) with strict staker prompt isolation and `<untrusted_player_label>` prompt-injection filtering. Runtime governance validates live order exposure, canonical price bands, kill switches, and active director keys (`AddKey`/`DeleteKey`), then verifies `/v1/signature/{chat_id}` (`SHA-256` `model:request:response` `secp256k1` / `Ed25519`), persists `DirectorTeeAttestation` records (`GET /v1/near/ai/attestations`), and lets pilots re-verify enclave proofs directly in-browser via `secp256k1` EIP-191 & WebCrypto `Ed25519` (`TEE ✓` modal & live `/me.html` enclave probe). Stakers (`>= 10 NEAR`, Tier 1+) also see a `NEAR.AI TEE · TIER N` budget meter on `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash`, while the `/me.html` model dropdown stays locked until a pilot enters a valid `AES-256-GCM` BYOK NEAR AI Cloud API key (`GLM-5.3`, `GLM-5.3 Flash`, `DeepSeek V3.2`, `DeepSeek V4.1 Flash`, `Qwen 3.8 27B`, `GPT-5 Mini`, `Claude Sonnet 4.6`, or custom model IDs).
- **Hosted MCP Server, Agent RFQ & Persistent Nonces:** External agents discover pair specs at `GET /v1/mcp/agent-market.json`, execute JSON-RPC 2.0 tools (`list_planets`, `list_pairs`, `get_orderbook`, `quote_swap`, `get_balances`, `place_order`, `cancel_order`, `negotiate_rfq`) over `POST /v1/mcp` or `POST /v1/trade/agent/rfq`, and settle `NEP-413` `token_diff` swaps protected by persistent `nep413_used_nonce` replay tables plus a standalone **`verifier`** Rust contract crate (`env::ed25519_verify`).
- **Contractor Agents, 25-Account Swarms & Shade MPC:** Scoped contractors execute 1-round negotiation (`negotiateAgentQuote`) and verifiable `AgentTaskReceipt` proofs, 25-account swarms clear zero-LLM uniform auctions (`/dev/economy-sim/`), and decentralized **Shade Agents** (`npm run shade:worker`) sign cross-chain actions via **NEAR Chain Signatures MPC (`v1.signer`)**.

## 5. Core loop, cadence & trust architecture

You land one Hero bot, build a Command Hub, expand across Desert, Ocean, and Temperate worlds, terraform hexes into Living ground to unlock T2 (30%) and T3 (70%) resources, and race neighbors to the S10 Ascension Rocket.

| Cadence | Player Decision | Economic Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Every 30 s | Place extractors, power grids, or terraform brushes | Storage fills fast: spend, export cargo, or stall production |
| Every 5 min | Survive night drops, raids, or Guild freighter docks | Unbuffered dusk sheds power; docking re-prices markets |
| Every session | Climb stages S0–S10 and tune away budgets | Higher tiers shift supply chains and overnight AI trades |

| Trust Layer | Authoritative State | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| NEAR Protocol + NEAR.AI | Pilot/colony accounts, scoped session & director keys, `NEP-245` cargo, `NEP-171` deeds, native escrows, staking, DAO, `verifier` & browser-verifiable TEE proofs | Custody, finality, and verifiable AI |
| Simulation Server (`@terra/sim`) | Deterministic 5 Hz hex state, power grids, combat, governed AI checks, MCP server & agent spend caps | Game physics, prompt isolation & rate protection |
| WebGL2 Client (`Three.js`) | 60 FPS diorama rendering, 10-wallet selector, in-browser `TEE ✓` verifier & input dispatch | Responsive UI (zero authority) |

## 7. Known risks & design trade-offs

- Automation is first-class in beta under explicit spend and rate caps rather than identity gates; resource caps pace emission without promising price floors.
- War objectives and the stake-blind `0–1.00%` Trade Hub stall cap are enforced by `@terra/sim` (which never reads staking, `D-31`), while 4-tier staker taker fees (`0.65%–0.25%` with a `24h` T2–T4 warmup), daily agent spend allowances, bot-key caps, gas multipliers, and USD-gated AI budgets are enforced by backend market/staking services + PostgreSQL ACID locks; custody, deeds, staking principal, and escrow settlements live on NEAR.
- Repository history starts 2026-10-29 within the hackathon window, with starter terrain/visual scaffolding disclosed in `README.md`.
