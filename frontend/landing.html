01 · THE LOOP

You give orders once.
The colony works around the clock.

Indirect control, not micromanagement. You set a task queue and four priorities. Robots mine, build, defend and trade on the server, every tick, whether your tab is open or not.

01

Pick an open planet

First time? You see every planet still taking colonies. Each holds 5 colonies and stops taking new ones after 24 hours, so you never land next to a week-old empire.

02

Drop the founder

One robot, a Command Hub and a small stock of all five resources. Its first job is to claim territory, because nothing can be built off your own tiles.

03

Set priorities

Economy, Expand, Defense, Rocket. The rule engine turns them into build orders. The AI Director steps in only when something big happens.

04

Leave. Come back.

Close the tab. Your colony keeps mining and repairing. When you return you drop straight back in, with a report of everything that happened while you were away.

05 · THE AI DIRECTOR

Rules for routine.
An AI for emergencies.

Most decisions come from a rule engine that already knows your planet, hazards and resource map. The AI Director is called only on big events: a critical shortage, a raid, a new neighbour, the rocket decision. That's at most once every 5 minutes per colony.

09 · FAQ

Questions from orbit

What happens to my colony when I close the tab?

It keeps running on the server. The rule engine follows your last priorities, the AI Director handles emergencies, and builders repair what storms break. Next time you open the game you land straight in your colony.

Why can't I join any planet I like?

Planets stop taking colonies once they have 5 or are a day old. That keeps every new player on a fair start. A fresh planet of each type is always kept ready.

Do I need a crypto wallet?

No. You start as a guest. Linking a NEAR wallet is optional and only moves your colony between devices.

How does a game end?

Build a rocket, launch it, and carry part of your colony to a new planet in the sector. A planet itself ends after 14 days or when every colony on it is gone.
