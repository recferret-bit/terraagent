## Goal and approved split

W11 is a small playable version of [W27](./W27-planet-sectors.md), using the same world model. Exactly three canonical worlds exist, one per D-33 type: Kharon Dunes (`desert` / `dune`), Thalassa Verde (`ocean` / `oceanForest`), Granite Hollow (`temperate` / `forestRock`). They persist without automatic age-based admission closure, lifetime expiry or all-settled/extinct ending. Capacity is allocated to founding seats and safe landing sites inside sectors. Full worlds queue players; they never create overflow instances or warm pools.

| W11 foundation | W27 expansion |
|---|---|
| 19-sector small atlas (`7` land + `12` ocean halo sectors, `R=48`, `7,057` tiles/sector, `134,083` total tiles) with stable tile/sector identities | Exact continent quotas (`150`–`450` sectors) and large atlas configurations |
| Sector admission, spawn recovery, parties, queue and resume | Same admission service tested at target scale |
| Isolated single-sector in-game play (`PlanetState.grid.radius = 48`, `7,057` tiles; cross-sector movement, construction, territory, power and terraforming blocked with `reason: 'cross_sector_disabled'`) | Infinite/streaming multi-sector loading, seamless cross-sector boundary traversal, bounded storage, pathing and cross-border recovery at scale |
| Simple planet cards, Orbital Picker and W04 Global Map (`M`) displaying all `19` surrounding sectors (`7` land + `12` ocean) | Hierarchical summaries, globe overlays and continuous ground-to-globe zoom |
| Versioned saves and bounded single-sector (`R=48`, `7,057` tiles) full-state replication | Partial replicas, interest subscriptions, sector checkpoints and measured large-world load |

**Removed from the current product:** five-seat planet instances, New Planet action, warm pools, overflow creation, 24-hour sealing, 14-day expiry, two-hour refill and automatic ending on extinction/settlement. Historical W11 issue IDs are retained below with explicitly revised work, not a second implementation of these policies. Pre-W11 dev seating remains a compatibility mode only.

## Small-world contract

- **Planning fixture:** one connected continent with seven land-bearing sectors and twelve surrounding ocean halo sectors (`19` sectors total) per type, radius `R=48` (`7,057` tiles/sector — 4× the previous `R=24` / `1,801`-tile hex area; `134,083` total tiles across the 19-sector small-atlas manifest: `49,399` land tiles + `84,684` ocean tiles; `W11_MAX_SMALL_ATLAS_TILES = 140,000`), `X=4` founding seats/sector: `28` nominal seats per planet before safe-site validation. Exercise `X=3/4/5` across all three planet profiles. These numbers are implementation defaults, not approved balance or measured capacity; see [WE-33](../../questions-and-limitations/sim-world-engine.md#we-33--w11-small-world-rollout-and-persistent-seat-policies).
- Use an exact integer macro-hex partition with unique tile ownership, symmetric neighbours and global axial coordinates. Gameplay sectors are distinct from the existing 8×8 storage buckets and render chunks; never repurpose `SECTOR_TILES`.
- Pin seed, generator/topology/config versions, bounds and config hash. Use existing biome generators and shared placement/locomotion within the active `R=48` (`7,057`-tile) sector. Validate safe, separated landing sites and starter resources in every land sector. Ocean sectors have no founding seats.
- Keep one authority and solar clock per planet. In W11, in-game `PlanetState.grid` loads only a **single isolated sector** of radius `R=48` (`7,057` tiles) rather than a multi-sector land cluster, and crossing sector boundaries in gameplay (unit movement/pathfinding, building placement across sectors or straddling seams, cross-sector power relay links, cross-sector territory claims, and cross-sector seam terraforming) is blocked (`reason: 'cross_sector_disabled'`). Both the Orbital Picker (pre-drop sector selection) and the in-game Global Map (`M` dialog) continue to display all `19` surrounding sectors (`7` land + `12` ocean) from the `AtlasManifestV1` / `SectorPartition` metadata. Infinite/streaming multi-sector loading and seamless cross-sector boundary traversal are deferred to [W27](./W27-planet-sectors.md).
- Active single-sector state (`R=48`, `7,057` tiles) and the 19-sector `AtlasManifestV1` metadata remain resident and replicated in W11, with explicit validated tile/population limits (`W11_MAX_SMALL_ATLAS_TILES = 140,000`). Fix identity/fog limits before exceeding eight colonies. Do not market this bounded baseline as sector streaming. W27 owns multi-sector streaming, sparse residency, cross-sector boundary traversal, bounded path scratch and partial replicas.
- Keep shared read/query and versioned atlas interfaces usable by W27. Preserve W11 saves, IDs and occupied terrain when enabling W27. A small atlas remains small; enabling W27 never changes its seed, bounds or topology. Larger new-world configurations require an explicit deployment/migration decision, never an automatic reset.

## Admission, persistence and player flow

New player → existing orbital menu → choose planet and sector (or explicitly choose automatic sector placement on that planet) → reserve slot → existing spawn saga → deploy/descent → colony. Returning players resume the authorized colony without another reservation. Keep all three world cards visible with open/full/paused/faulted status and timestamped counts; no New Planet card or cosmetic population estimates.

Separate founded/reserved seats, colonies physically present and players online. Moving, disconnecting, panning or unloading visuals changes no founding-seat count. Lock sector capacity and landing sites together; validate again at sim authority; deduplicate by ticket/colony IDs. A stale chosen sector offers another sector on the same planet or queues; never silently switches worlds. Retain backpressure, cancellation, priority, reservation TTL, retries and orphan reconciliation.

**Persistent-life policy:** no clock or population transition automatically seals/ends a canonical world. Full is derived availability, not permanent lifecycle sealing. Admin pause/resume and explicit maintenance archival remain audited operations. Restart does not manufacture a replacement world. Offline colonies keep ordinary W09 behavior when enabled.

**Conservative unresolved policies (WE-33):** expired/failed reservations release exactly once after orphan reconciliation; founded seats remain held after elimination or settlement until a separately approved reclaim policy exists. Do not evict inactive players or recycle a ruined landing site. Keep the existing no-rejoin-on-the-same-planet restriction pending a persistent-world decision. Transfers keep carry pending if no eligible different planet is selected; no same-source respawn or automatic instance creation. These are safe planning defaults, not new owner-approved balance rules.

Parties use one intent, atomic adjacent slot reservations in the same or neighbouring sectors, one colony per member, and no split across planets. If adjacency/capacity cannot be met, queue the entire unassigned party. Partial spawn failure keeps successful colonies and reconciles/releases only failed members; UI reports partial completion. Party maximum remains five independently of sector capacity.

## Demo story

1. Open `/play.html?scenario=w11` as a new guest: exactly three world cards with 19-sector fixtures (`7` land sectors + `12` ocean halo sectors, `R=48`, `7,057` tiles/sector, `134,083` total tiles), sector capacity and live counts; select a planet and sector on the Orbital Picker.
2. Race five guests into an X=4 sector: four assigned, fifth queued or explicitly offered another sector on the same planet. World count remains three. Join → existing descent into the reserved safe site.
3. Inspect the active isolated sector (`PlanetState.grid.radius = 48`, `7,057` tiles) and attempt to walk, place a building footprint, link power, claim territory or terraform across a sector boundary: cross-sector actions are rejected (`reason: 'cross_sector_disabled'`), while opening the Global Map (`M`) displays all `19` surrounding sectors (`7` land + `12` ocean) with biome/resource summaries and colony presence badges. Seamless cross-sector traversal and streaming are deferred to W27.
4. Reload queued and active sessions; retry spawn timeouts and restart the app: same intent/colony/world, no duplicate reservation or lost terrain edits.
5. Join a party across sectors; inject partial spawn failure and expiry: successful members stay, only failed reservations release. Verify cross-sector beacon/territory isolation in W11 (`cross_sector_disabled`), with multi-sector territory expansion deferred to W27.
6. Advance age past 24 hours and 14 days, and eliminate all test colonies: worlds remain persistent; no warm/overflow instance, age seal or automatic reset. Founded seats stay held under the provisional reclaim policy.
7. Fill all safe slots: full cards remain visible, queue is cancellable, active players still resume. Pause/resume admission and verify exactly-once reservation release.
8. Disable W11 and run pre-W11 scenarios: old dev seating remains. Attempt to open an atlas save with an incompatible flag/client: clear error, no regeneration.
9. Run local-host fixture for single-sector (`R=48`, `7,057` tiles) + 19-sector manifest generation, intra-sector walking/building, cross-sector boundary guard (`cross_sector_disabled`) and save parity; online-only matchmaking is explicitly unavailable locally.
