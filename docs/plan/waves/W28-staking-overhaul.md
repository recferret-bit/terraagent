## Goal

Turn TerraAgent's staking into a **3-way win** for players, the NEAR ecosystem, and protocol profitability: **Wallet/Pilot-level 4-Tier Orbital Patronage (`10 / 50 / 200 / 500+ NEAR`)**, a **`/landing.html` → `/me.html#staking`** funnel with a public unauthenticated preview, **5 Free `GLM-5.3-Flash` TEE Trial Turns** for verified pilots, an **"Effective APY" Wealth, AI Budget & Ecosystem Dashboard** on `/me.html#staking` (with 1-click **Compound Yield**, **Stake Trade Profits**, NEAR Validator/LST TVL strip, and **Shareable Orbital Patron Cards**), 1-tx Native `NEAR` + two-way **`stNEAR`/`LiNEAR`** share-price-solvent staking with instant single-step withdrawals, anti-snipe fee rules (`1.00% → 0.65% instant → 0.50% / 0.35% / 0.25%` after `24h` warmup + `0.25%` `<7d` early-exit fee), wallet-shared dynamic USD `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` compute budgets (`Cost ≤ 35%` revenue) + **Concurrent `NEAR.AI` Colony Slots (`1 / 3 / 6 / 12`)**, **King-of-the-Hill Tier 4 Planet Sponsorship**, 3D HQ Orbital Uplink Beam + Flagship Carrier prestige, plus `/trade.html` `RampView` decimal scaling and `verifier.terraagent.testnet` deployment.

## Demo story (wave e2e)

1. **Landing Conversion Funnel:** Open `/landing.html` → see the top-nav **`⚡ STAKE · LIVE APY`** pill. Drag the `#economics` slider (`0–500 NEAR`) across the 4 tier markers → fee savings (`1.00% → 0.65% / 0.50% / 0.35% / 0.25%`) update live and the primary CTA **`STAKE <X> NEAR · UNLOCK TIER <N> →`** routes to `/me.html#staking?amount=<X>`.
2. **Public Staking Preview (Zero Login Wall):** Open `/me.html#staking?amount=50` logged out → see live validator APY (or clearly badged `Sim APY` on Testnet/Mock), the 4-Tier matrix, pre-filled `50 NEAR` income projections, NEAR Ecosystem TVL scoreboard, and **`Connect NEAR Wallet to Stake 50 NEAR`**.
3. **5-Turn Free `NEAR.AI TEE` Trial → Seamless Stake Prompt:** Sign in as a verified pilot (`!isGuest`) with `0 NEAR` staked and open `/play.html` → `DirectorPanel` displays **`FREE TEE TRIAL (5 / 5 TURNS LEFT · GLM-5.3-Flash)`** with real `TEE ✓` proofs. After 5 turns, it shifts cleanly to **`SCRIPT AI (FREE)`** with an inline **`Trial Complete — Stake 10 NEAR → Keep NEAR.AI TEE Active`** CTA (anonymous guests skip the trial to prevent bot farming).
4. **Wallet-First Staking, Compounding & Wealth Dashboard:** Stake `50 NEAR` (or `stNEAR`/`LiNEAR`) on `/me.html#staking` with zero founded colonies → `0.65%` Tier 1 fee unlocks at `t=0` while the `24h` warmup badge counts down to Tier 2's `0.50%` fee; KPIs show Total Staked (`NEAR` + `USD`), live ticking yield + **`[↻ Compound Yield]`** (`0%` fee), Effective Combined APY % (excluding self-fills), **`NEAR.AI` Colony Slots (`0 / 3`)**, dynamic **`⚡ AI Compute Budget`**, hybrid SVG `30d`/`90d`/`1y` chart, and **Shareable Orbital Patron Badge** (`X` / `NEAR Social`).
5. **Anti-Snipe Early Exit, Instant Downgrade & Direct Withdrawal:** Unstake `45 NEAR` within `< 7 days` → UI previews and contract routes the `0.25%` (`25 bp`) early-unstake fee to `protocol.terraagent.<root>` (`0%` after `≥ 7 days`), and tier immediately downgrades to Tier 0 (`unbonding` excluded). After unbonding (`warp 2d`), **`Withdraw to Wallet`** transfers native `NEAR` or LSTs directly to the user's wallet in one step.
6. **Wallet-Shared Quotas, King-of-the-Hill Tier 4 & 3D Prestige (`D-31`):** Scale stake across `10 / 50 / 200 / 500+ NEAR` → wallet-wide `NEP-366` gas & dynamic USD `glm-5.3-flash` budgets (`Cost ≤ 35%` even if `NEAR/USD` drops) share across up to `1 / 3 / 6 / 12` active TEE colonies; on a public planet, staking `550 NEAR` overtakes a `500 NEAR` rival as **King-of-the-Hill Lead Patron** (`15%` of Protocol's fee share + **`ISS <pilot>.near`** Flagship Carrier in the 3D Orbit Menu skybox + HQ Cyan Orbital Uplink Beam), while `@terra/sim` remains 100% zero-P2W.
7. **"Stake Trade Profits" & `RampView` Decimal Fix (`/trade.html`):** Sell cargo for `USDC` on `/trade.html` → click **`[⚡ Stake Trade Profits]`** (`USDC → stNEAR` & stake), verify header fee pill (`0.65%..0.25%`), and quote `$10.00 USDC` (`6` dec → `10000000`) in `RampView` with zero `$1 quintillion` overflow (`dry: true` on Testnet).
8. **On-Chain `verifier` Deployment & Key Sync:** Verify `verifier.terraagent.testnet` deployment, `register_account_key` sync (`all_keys_onchain == true`, `strict_balances == true`), and explicit RPC error reporting.

   ```bash
   git worktree add ../TerraAgent-wt/w28 -b wave/w28-staking-overhaul main
   cd ../TerraAgent-wt/w28
   npm ci
   ```

   Allocate an isolated port & DB block in `infra/.env.worktree` (matching `scripts/wave/isolation.mjs`, e.g. `TA_PORT_BASE=4200` and `TA_DB_NAME=terra_wave_w28_staking_overhaul` after verifying unused bases across `../TerraAgent-wt/*`).
