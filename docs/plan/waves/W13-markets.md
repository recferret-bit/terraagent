## Goal

Every Trade Hub is a market stall. USDC is the default quote; barter and optional NEAR quotes remain supported. The buyer pays the full resource notional plus a 1% platform fee and a stall fee of 0–1% (default 1%), all in the quote token. The maker's stall earns the stall fee. Price-time matching and mock Intents settlement conserve every asset. Database-managed profiles configure production, consumption, recipes, fees and agents; reproducible experiments evaluate candidates using the same shared rules as gameplay.

**Decisions (D-44):**
- Custodial accounts allow partial fills; claimed accounts use all-or-nothing orders.
- Buyer pays quote-token fees regardless of maker/taker role. Orders pin policy/fee versions and reserve the full inclusive cost. Cumulative exact rounding, minimum notional and atomic reservation rules are defined in ECONOMY §3.
- Same-colony orders do not match. A fill is organic only when both sides are actor kind `player` or `bot`, different colonies and different owners (D-45); same-owner, fixture, subsidy, `npc` and Guild fills never are. No anti-bot identity programme is added; bots are first-class participants bounded by allowances (D-45).
- External cargo transfers remain open. Our order books are planet-local, but Guild transport is not exclusive; external venue fee leakage is accepted.
- Scarcity comes from useful consumption and production constraints, not a promised resource price. All calibration assumptions and real-money gates are explicit.

**Bots and humans on one book (D-45):** an API key bound to a colony account lets an LLM-controlled or scripted bot place orders through `/v1/trade/*` (command source `'bot'`), under allowances (max open orders, order notional, daily spend/loss budget, allowed pairs, price band versus reference, kill switch, idempotency, rate limits). The LLM sets strategy only; the pure need/value quote library (`shared/sim/src/systems/trade/agent/`) prices within bounds and never accepts a free LLM price. Bot-only markets can establish a reference price; bot demand is credible only if the bot consumes what it buys (hypothesis, LED-18).

**Resources (D-34):** every tradable sub-resource (`ResourceKey`, 10 keys) is its own market pair against each quote token (another `ResourceKey`, NEAR or USDC). T1 pairs trade from day one, and a colony can buy T2/T3 with T1 before it can mine them. Energy is not tradable. In the steps below, "water" is `bio.water` and "metals" is `metals.t1`.

## Demo story (the e2e in words)

1. Open `/play.html?scenario=w13&as=a`; use the join link for B in another profile. A has 600 water; B has 20 mock USDC; both have online Trade Hubs. Mock balances are labeled.
2. A opens Market, selects water/USDC and sets its stall fee to 1%.
3. A offers 300 water for 10 USDC, TTL 1 h. The order pins the policy and reserves 300 water.
4. B previews 300 water, 10 USDC notional, 0.10 platform fee, 0.10 stall fee, total 10.20. On fill B receives all 300 water; seller A earns 10 USDC and A's stall separately earns 0.10; platform earns 0.10. The explorer shows an atomic zero-sum batch with separate fee attribution even when account legs are aggregated.
5. Price board shows the gross execution price and organic/fixture classification; insufficient eligible history is labeled and cannot drive real cash bot bids.
6. Partial-fill a second order in multiple lots; cumulative notional/fees equal the corresponding unsplit fill within each policy bucket. Exercise a resting buy with incoming sell: the buyer still pays.
7. Cancel the remaining order and verify principal plus fee reservations release exactly once.
8. Inject a final settlement failure: balances stay unchanged, orders/reservations recover atomically. An unknown outcome remains pending until reconciliation. Restart after execution but before recording final: one trade only.
9. Open the planned operator economy panel, clone the baseline profile, edit a recipe/fee field, validate and run a seeded baseline/candidate comparison. Review gates and diff; activate for this mock sandbox, reload, then roll back. Orders already open keep their terms.
10. Complete a recurring prestige project through the existing queue, export/import cargo with the transport recipe, and inspect actual resource consumption separately from representation transfers. Run flat-population and supply/demand-shock experiments; show per-resource flows and fee contribution with no simulated revenue presented as real revenue.
11. Run `TA_WAVES=..12 npm run dev:restart`: Market and W13 economy actions disappear; earlier gameplay and pinned legacy data behave as before. Persisted orders and experiment records remain intact.
12. (D-45) Create a bot API key for colony B in Settings, then run `npm run trade:bot -- --key <key> --strategy scripted` (scripted trader on the quote library). Open `/trade.html` as A: the bot's bids/asks appear in the same depth as A's, and A's orders, fills and orbital cargo (only mine) are listed. A fills a bot order and the bot fills A's; the board marks each fill organic or not (player/bot, different colonies and owners). Send a bot order above its notional limit, outside its price band, and repeat an idempotency key: stable error codes, one order. Hit the kill switch: bot orders cancel and reservations release once. Run the scenario with the emulated chain's fault injection (unknown outcome, delayed finality, duplicate delivery, insufficient funds): balances stay conserved and nothing executes twice.
