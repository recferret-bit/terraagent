# W03 — The Grid: owner check

> The wave's demo story ([`waves/W03-the-grid.md`](../waves/W03-the-grid.md)) as a hands-on checklist. Format and rules: [`README.md`](./README.md). About 15 minutes.

## Before you start

- **Where it runs:** branch `wave/w03-the-grid`, worktree `../TerraAgent-wt/w03`. Your main checkout is untouched.
- **Ports:** this worktree's `infra/.env.worktree` sets `TA_PORT_BASE=4100` → app **4101**, client **5273** (`TA_DB_NAME=terra_wave_w03_the_grid`). Below, `<client>` = `http://127.0.0.1:5273`, `<app>` = `http://127.0.0.1:4101`.
- **Start the stack** (if it isn't running): `cd ../TerraAgent-wt/w03 && npm run dev` (Docker must be running for Postgres). Stop it with Ctrl-C, or `kill $(cat scripts/dev/.out/runner.pid)` if it was started in the background.
- **Dev console:** in the game page press **`` ` ``** (the key under Esc). A dark panel opens at the bottom; type commands there and press Enter. It is **not** the browser's DevTools console. Click the game first if DevTools has focus. `tune` needs the game server (it does not work with `?host=local`).
- **Menus & queue shortcuts:** the menus are tabs of one Command Dock (bottom-left, `HIDE` / `SHOW`): press **`B`** (or click **`BUILD [B]`**) for the Construction menu, **`U`** (or click **`PRODUCTION [U]`**) for the Units menu, **`T`** (or click **`TERRAFORM [T]`**) for the Terraform tools, and click **`QUEUE`** for the Order Queue (**`Q`** collapses / expands it).
- **Hex coordinates** below are `(q, r)` with the Command Hub at `(0, 0)`, Metals Extractor at `(3, 0)`, Solar Array at `(-3, 2)`, HQ drone at `(2, -1)` and two Harvesters at `(2, 2)` and `(-2, 2)`.
- Every tool used below is described in [`docs/dev/DEV_TOOLS.md`](../../dev/DEV_TOOLS.md).

## Steps

1. **Morning colony from the end of W02.** Open `<client>/play.html?scenario=w03` → a temperate hex planet loads in the morning with a finished Command Hub T1 at `(0, 0)`, a Metals Extractor T1 at `(3, 0)`, a Solar Array T1 at `(-3, 2)`, the HQ drone at `(2, -1)` and 2 Harvesters at `(2, 2)` and `(-2, 2)`. In the top bar **see** the three family chips — **Metals `400 / 500`**, **Minerals `250 / 500`**, **Bio `400 / 500`** (`bio.biomass 200`, `bio.water 200`) — the **Supply** chip, the **energy chip** (`800 / 800 E`, hover shows **`Income (Supply): +28 E/s`**, **`Spending (Demand): −15 E/s`**, **`Net: +13 E/s`**) and the planet clock **`☀ 06:00 100%`**.
2. **Produce a Builder drone at the Hub and run 2 constructions in parallel.** Press **`U`** (or click **`PRODUCTION [U]`**) to open the Units menu, select **Builder Drone** (`55 M · 25 Min · 10 Bio · 12 s · 1.0× build · 1.5× repair`) and click **QUEUE**. Type `warp 10` in the dev console → after ~1.2 s **see** the Builder roll out from the Hub edge. Now press **`B`** (or click **`BUILD [B]`**), select **Power Pylon T1** (`15 M · 5 Min · 6 s`), click `(4, -2)`, then select **Power Pylon T1** again and click `(-4, -2)` → **see** the HQ drone and the Builder drone each take one site and build both pylons in parallel. Then run `warp 1`.
3. **Power-Line drag tool (3 pylons = 1 queue entry).** Press **`B`** to open the Construction menu, select **Power Line Pylon T1** (`15 M · 5 Min · 6 s · Span 6 · Max 80 E/s`), and drag (or click start `(3, -1)` then end `(9, -1)`) along the east corridor toward the distant minerals deposit at `(10, 0)` → **see** the ghost preview **3 auto-spaced pylon sub-sites** (`(3, -1)`, `(6, -1)`, `(9, -1)`) with the total cost (`45 M · 15 Min`), and in the **QUEUE** tab **see** a single Construction lane entry **`Power Line (×3)`**. With `warp 10` the workers build all 3 pylons along the line. *(Note: the `P` power overlay is `W03-18` (`H+`, deferred); in the H slice the synced power graph is inspected via the top-right building inspector or the `power` dev console command.)*
4. **Distant Minerals Quarry: `offline: no grid` → online when connected.** First, on a fresh load of `<client>/play.html?scenario=w03` (or before building the east power line in step 3), press **`B`**, select **Minerals Quarry T1** (`75 M · 30 Min · 15 W · 16 s · −7 E/s`), and place it on the distant minerals deposit at **`(10, 0)`** (beyond the Hub's 3-tile connection radius). Run `warp 20` until it finishes → click the Quarry at `(10, 0)` and **see** the top-right building inspector read **`OFFLINE: NO GRID`** (`offline: no grid`). Now drag/place the 3-pylon Power Line from `(3, -1)` to `(9, -1)` (as in step 3) → as soon as the chain finishes, **see** the Quarry inspector switch to **`ONLINE`** and the Minerals chip income rise by **`+0.75/s`**.
5. **Supply Depot raises every family cap from `500` to `1 500` (+ Wind & Capacitor).** Press **`B`**, select **Supply Depot T1** (`80 M · 60 Min · 10 Bio · 14 s · −1 E/s · +1 000 cap`) and place it at **`(-3, -3)`** (with `warp 20` if desired). When it completes → **see** the top-bar family chips change from **`/ 500`** to **`/ 1500`** across **Metals**, **Minerals** and **Bio**. You can also place a **Wind Turbine T1** at `(0, -4)` and a **Capacitor Bank T1** at `(3, -3)` → run `power` in the dev console to see wind generation and capacitor stored energy (`1 500 E` max) rise.
6. **Night brownout and shedding by group (`group 1` first, then `group 2`, `group 3`…).** In the dev console run `time night` → **see** the clock switch to **`☾`**, Solar drop to `0`, and any charged Capacitor discharge up to `30 E/s` while Wind holds. Now run `addLoad 200` in the dev console → **see** demand exceed supply, the red banner top-right read **`BROWNOUT — SHEDDING GROUP … · N E/s OFFLINE`**, and clicking a shed building (such as the Quarry or Metals Extractor) show **`OFFLINE: BROWNOUT`** (`offline: brownout`) in the top-right building inspector. Reset with `addLoad 0` and `time day`.
7. **Per-pylon max load (`> 80 E/s` on a T1 pylon) & branch overload.** Place a **Power Pylon T1** at **`(5, -1)`** and a **Water Extractor T1** at **`(7, -1)`** (so the Water Extractor attaches to that pylon branch). Once both are built, run `addLoad 85` in the dev console (pushing the T1 pylon over its `80 E/s` limit while keeping the Hub under `150 E/s`) → **see** the top-right banner switch to **`POWER OVERLOAD — PYLON CAPACITY EXCEEDED`**, the pylon inspector show **`OVERLOAD`** with its flow (`91 / 80 E/s`), and the downstream Water Extractor show **`OFFLINE: OVERLOAD`** (`offline: overload`). Reset with `addLoad 0`.
8. **Building entity ID in the selection panel & auto-queued `1.5×` Builder repair.** Click the **Metals Extractor** at **`(3, 0)`** → in the bottom selection bar **see** its entity ID badge **`#2`** (`[data-testid="selection-entity-id"]`) next to its title (`METALS EXTRACTOR T1`). In the dev console run `damage 2 60` (damaging entity `#2` by 60 %, down to `200 / 500 HP`) → **see** its HP bar drop to 40 %, an auto-queued **`Repair #2`** entry appear in the Construction lane at `system` precedence, and the Builder drone walk over and repair it back to **`500 / 500 HP`** at its `1.5×` repair rate (`warp 10` speeds it up).
9. **`waiting: storage` and the interactive `Build Supply Depot` hint button.** Open a fresh `<client>/play.html?scenario=w03` (where the family caps are still `500`), open the dev console and run (with single quotes):
   `tune economyT1Basic.solar.cost '{"metals.t1":600,"minerals.t1":75,"bio.biomass":10,"bio.water":5}'`
   Then press **`B`**, select **Solar Collector T1** and place it at **`(5, -4)`**. Click the **`QUEUE`** tab in the Command Dock → **see** the entry show **`waiting: storage (no ETA)`** and an action button **`Build Supply Depot (+1 000 cap)`** (`[data-testid="queue-storage-hint"]`). Click **`Build Supply Depot (+1 000 cap)`** → **see** the Command Dock switch to the **`BUILD`** tab with **Supply Depot** selected and the Supply Depot placement ghost active on the map. Cancel the test entry (`×`) and run `tune reset`.
10. **Four lane tabs (`ALL`, `BUILD`, `TERRA`, `UNITS`, `HQ`) & 60 s resource-starved earmark.** In the **`QUEUE`** tab of the Command Dock, **see** the lane filter tabs (`ALL`, `BUILD`, `TERRA`, `UNITS`, `HQ`) with per-lane counts (`0/20`, `0/20`, `0/10`, `0/1`). In the dev console run `fill bio.water 0` (draining `bio.water` to `0`, while Harvesters only gather Metals and Minerals), then place a **Solar Collector T1** at **`(5, -4)`** (which needs `5 W`). It enters **`waiting: Bio 0/5`**. Run `warp 20` in the dev console → after 60 s of game time (~3 real seconds at `warp 20`), **see** the earmark bar (`[data-testid="queue-earmark-bar"]`) appear at the top of the Queue panel reading **`Earmarked: Water 5`** and a badge on the starved entry showing **`earmarked: Water 5`**, reserving that resource against lower-precedence orders. Run `warp 1`.
11. **Night Survived & Night Shift progression** *(deferred: `W03-15` and `W03-25` are outside the 15-issue H slice, recorded in [`cross-cutting.md`](../../questions-and-limitations/cross-cutting.md) XC-34).*
12. **Earlier waves stay clean (`?waves=..02`).** Open `<client>/play.html?scenario=w03&waves=..02` → **see** the W02 game without any W03 UI: no four-lane tab bar (`[data-testid="queue-lane-tabs"]`) in the Queue panel, no Pylon / Supply Depot / Minerals Quarry / Water Extractor / Thermal Plant in the Construction menu, and no Builder Drone in the Units menu.

## When you're done

- **All good:** from the main checkout run `npm run wave -- land 03`. It re-runs the gates, merges W03 into `main`, tags `wave-03`, pushes, and removes the W03 worktree and branch. (Plain `npm run wave` does the same and then opens the next ready wave.)
- **Something is wrong:** tell the wave lead which step and what you saw; it is fixed on the wave branch before anything lands.
