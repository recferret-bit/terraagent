# W02 — First Light: owner check

> The wave's demo story ([`waves/W02-first-light.md`](../waves/W02-first-light.md)) as a hands-on checklist. Format and rules: [`README.md`](./README.md). About 15 minutes.

## Before you start

- **Where it runs:** branch `wave/w02-first-light`, worktree `../TerraAgent-wt/w02`. Your main checkout is untouched.
- **Ports:** this worktree's `.env.worktree` sets `TA_PORT_BASE=4100` → app **4101**, client **5273** (`TA_DB_NAME=terra_wave_w02_first_light`). Below, `<client>` = `http://127.0.0.1:5273`, `<app>` = `http://127.0.0.1:4101`.
- **Start the stack** (if it isn't running): `cd ../TerraAgent-wt/w02 && npm run dev` (Docker must be running for Postgres). Stop it with Ctrl-C, or `kill $(cat scripts/dev/.out/runner.pid)` if it was started in the background.
- **Dev console:** in the game page press **`` ` ``** (the key under Esc). A dark panel opens at the bottom; type commands there and press Enter. It is **not** the browser's DevTools console. Click the game first if DevTools has focus. `tune` needs the game server (it does not work with `?host=local`).
- **Menus & queue shortcuts:** the menus are tabs of one Command Dock (bottom-left, `HIDE` / `SHOW`): press **`B`** (or click **`BUILD [B]`**) for the Construction menu, **`U`** (or click **`PRODUCTION [U]`**) for the Units menu, **`T`** (or click **`TERRAFORM [T]`**) for the Terraform tools, and click **`QUEUE`** for the Order Queue (**`Q`** collapses / expands it).
- **Hex coordinates** below are `(q, r)` with the Command Hub at `(0, 0)`; the HQ drone starts at `(2, -1)`.
- Every tool used below is described in [`docs/dev/DEV_TOOLS.md`](../../dev/DEV_TOOLS.md).

## Steps

1. **Morning colony, family resource bar, energy chip & planet clock.** Open `<client>/play.html?scenario=w02` → a temperate hex planet loads in the morning with a finished Command Hub T1 at `(0, 0)`, the HQ drone next to it and surface metals, minerals and oil deposits nearby. In the top bar **see** three family chips — **Metals `400 / 500`**, **Minerals `250 / 500`**, **Bio `300 / 500`** (hover a chip for its T1/T2/T3 breakdown: `metals.t1 400`, `minerals.t1 250`, `bio.biomass 150`, `bio.water 150`) — a **Supply** chip, plus the **energy chip** (the HQ power cell, a small bar and `200 / 200 E`). Right after it, **see** the planet clock `☀ 06:00 100%`. Hover the energy chip to see `HQ Cell: 200 / 200 E`, **`Income (Supply): +8 E/s`**, **`Spending (Demand): −5 E/s`** and **`Net: +3 E/s`**; a `Brownout: N E/s offline` line appears only while consumers are shed. Top right stays empty apart from the brownout banner (step 6) and the building inspector.
2. **Produce a Worker Drone T1 from the Hub.** Press **`U`** (or click **`PRODUCTION [U]`**) to open the Units menu, select **Worker Drone T1** (`40 M · 15 Min · 5 Bio · 10 s`) and click **QUEUE** → **see** the Units lane entry in the Order Queue go **`IN PROGRESS`**, and stock drop gradually while it builds (paid as progress is made, `40/15/5/0` in total: `Metals 360`, `Minerals 235`, `Bio 295` when it is done). About 10 s later (or ~1 s with `warp 10` in the dev console) a Worker Drone T1 rolls out from the Hub edge and starts harvesting the nearest surface deposit by itself (`+0.5/s`).
3. **Place a Metals Extractor and a Solar Collector.** Press **`B`** (or click **`BUILD [B]`**) to open the Construction menu:
   1. Select **Metals Extractor T1** (`65 M · 20 Min · 10 W · 14 s · −6 E/s`). Hover near the metals deposit at `(3, 0)` — off the deposit the ghost turns red (`must cover a metals deposit`); on `(3, 0)` the deposit tiles glow and the ghost shows the prep bill. Click to place.
   2. Select **Solar Collector T1** (`60 M · 75 Min · 10 Bio · 5 W · 18 s · +20 E/s`) and click free ground near the Hub, e.g. `(-3, 2)` (a Solar may not stand on a deposit).
   3. Type `warp 10` in the dev console. **See** both orders in the Construction lane, one building after the other: the second shows `waiting: worker` while the first is under way; the active one shows `waiting: worker (HQ en route)` while the HQ drone walks over, `waiting: site (prep n/4)` while it preps the ground, then builds; when it is done the HQ walks to the second site and does the same. At `warp 10` the first is done after about 2 s and both after about 5 s (about 19 s and 43 s at normal speed). Then `warp 1`.
4. **Extractor income and Solar power.** Once both buildings finish → **see** the Metals chip income rise by **`+1.0/s`** while the Extractor is powered, and the energy chip's hover show **`Income (Supply): +28 E/s`** (the Hub's 8 plus the Solar's 20). In the dev console type `power` → the printout's supply line shows **`solar: 20 [gross 21, drain 1]`**.
5. **Queue waiting on missing resources + live ETA.** In the dev console run `fill minerals.t1 5` (sets `minerals.t1` to `5`, below the Extractor's `20 Min` cost), then place a second **Metals Extractor T1** on the free metals deposit at **`(6, 0)`** (anchor it at `(5, -1)` so it covers `(6, 0)`; `(4, 0)` is inside the first Extractor) → **see** **`waiting: Minerals 5/20 · ~30 s`** on the order in the Order Queue, the ETA counting down while a Worker Drone T1 brings Minerals in (it shows `waiting: Minerals 5/20 (no ETA)` while nothing produces Minerals). When Minerals reach `20` (or at once with `fill minerals.t1 250`), **see** the order start by itself.
6. **Night brownout and sunrise recovery.** In the dev console run `time night` (18:00) → **see** the clock chip turn to `☾`, Solar output drop to `0` (its `−1 E/s` drain stays), demand exceed supply, a red banner top right — **`BROWNOUT — SHEDDING GROUP 3 · 6 E/s OFFLINE`** (or **`BROWNOUT — DEMAND OVER SUPPLY`** while nothing is shed yet) — the building inspector show the Extractor **`offline: brownout`**, and the energy chip's hover a `Brownout: 6 E/s offline` line. Run `time day` (12:00) or `skipnight` (06:00) → **see** daylight and the Solar's `+20 E/s` back, the banner gone and the Extractor producing again. (Without commands, night falls by itself about 7.5 real minutes after `skipnight`: one planet day is 15 real minutes.)
7. **Storage cap halt and `waiting(storage)`.** In the dev console run `fill metals.t1` (or `fill metals.t1 9999`) → **see** the Metals chip at **`500 / 500`** (highlighted at cap) and the Extractor stop adding Metals. Then run (single quotes as written):
   `tune economyT1Basic.solar.cost '{"metals.t1":600,"minerals.t1":75,"bio.biomass":10,"bio.water":5}'`
   and place a **Solar Collector T1** at **`(5, -4)`** → after a moment **see** its queue entry show **`waiting: storage (no ETA)`** with a greyed hint **`Build a Supply Depot to raise the cap (W03)`** (the Supply Depot arrives in W03). Cancel that entry (its **`×`**) and run `tune reset`.
8. **Cancel refunds (50 % of what was paid in progress vs free while waiting).** Run `fill metals.t1 300` first: a refund never lifts a family above its cap, so at `500 / 500` the Metals part of a refund would be lost. Place a **Solar Collector T1** at **`(5, -4)`**; once the HQ has walked over and prepped the site it goes **`IN PROGRESS`** and pays `60 M / 75 Min / 10 Bio / 5 W` gradually. When it is about half built, click its **`×` (Cancel)** in the Order Queue → **see** **50 %** of what it has paid so far come back in the stock chips (at half built: about `+15 M`, `+19 Min`). Then run `fill minerals.t1 5`, place another Solar at `(5, -4)` so it stays **`WAITING`**, and click **`×`** → **see** it cancelled with nothing charged.
9. **Terraform tab, repair, resource find, hand lasers & particles.** Press **`T`** (or click **`TERRAFORM [T]`** in the dock) → **see** three buttons top to bottom: **`TERRAFORM [G]`**, **`REPAIR [R]`** (`40%` of base cost for a full repair, paid as it goes), **`DISMANTLE [Z]`** (refund `50%` of what was spent). The `G` / `R` / `Z` keys work only while this tab is open (on the Build tab they do nothing); **`X`** only stops the selected unit. The reference table below the buttons shows `Resource find · 1% per hex · t1 50% / t2 35% / t3 15%`, `Worker Drone T1 (per deposit) +0.50/s of its resource` and `Metals Extractor +1.00 Metals/s`.
   - Click **TERRAFORM** (or press `G`), then a tree or rock hex inside your territory (the scenario flattens everything within 8 hexes of the Hub, so the trees and rocks are in the outer ring of the territory, 9–10 hexes out) → the status reads `Terraform ordered: … · continues hex by hex`, the tool disarms (the next click selects normally), and the status line follows the chain (`TERRAFORM TREES (q,r) 18% · hex 2`). The HQ drone walks over, clears the hex, then **keeps going by itself** to the trees / rocks inside the territory nearest the hex you clicked, ring by ring, one hex at a time (`warp 10` speeds it up), and stops when none is left.
   - While it works (also from 1–2 hexes away), **see** two cutter lasers come from the drone's left and right hands (glowing palm orbs) and hit the tree / rock, a ring of trunk / foliage or boulder chunks fly out around it, and a blue progress bar above it. Cleared hexes look like plain flat grass.
   - Select the drone and press **`X`** → it stops and the chain ends; press **Esc**.
   - Click **REPAIR**, then empty ground → `Nothing to repair here`.
   - Dev console `tune terraformFinds.deposit.chance 1`, order TERRAFORM again → each finished hex finds a deposit: **see** a toast `Resource found: … (T<n>) deposit at (q, r)`; click the deposit → its card title ends in `· T<n>`; tier 2/3 clusters are bigger (4–5 nodes). `spawndeposit 2 2 metals 3` places one directly; `tune reset` restores the 1 % chance. A Worker Drone T1 on a T2/T3 metals deposit raises the `metals.t2` / `metals.t3` line of the Metals chip breakdown. The three tier looks side by side: `<client>/playground.html?inspect=robot.hero&biome=forestRock&view=iso&hexterrain=1`, zoom out, the row of three Metals clusters in front of the model (3 / 4 / 5 nodes).
10. **Earlier waves stay clean (`?waves=..01`).** Open `<client>/play.html?scenario=w02&waves=..01` → **see** the W01 game without the W02 UI: the energy chip is there but its hover has no Income / Spending / Net lines, no planet clock chip, no **`PRODUCTION`** tab, the Build tab shows only the W01 Hub menu (Command Hub, no Extractor or Solar), no brownout banner or building inspector top right, the Terraform tab offers only **`TERRAFORM`** (no REPAIR / DISMANTLE), and no overhead HP / progress bars over units. (Some W02 3D and dock details still show under `..01`; they are listed in [`frontend-features.md`](../../questions-and-limitations/frontend-features.md) FF-51.)
11. **Visual check: selection bar, 3-stage deposits, day/night lights & playground** (owner only, not in the e2e). Back on `<client>/play.html?scenario=w02`:
    - **Deposits:** click a surface deposit → **see** the bottom selection bar show its resource family, stage (`FULL` above 50 %, `HALF` 1–50 %, `DEPLETED` at 0 %, rubble) and `remaining / 500`. Produce up to 6 Worker Drones (T1) and right-click the same deposit → **see** them park on its 6 neighbouring hexes (never inside it) and harvest together while its 3D model goes from `full` to `half` to `depleted`.
    - **Day/night lights:** `time 12:00` → unit headlamps and building floodlights are off. `time 00:00` → building lights on all four sides and unit lights come on, with cool-blue moon shadows (**`F8`** → **`SHADOW`** tab, `SHADOW SOURCE MODE` = `SUN + NIGHT MOON`). These lights follow the W01 viewport day/night clock, which syncs from the planet's solar clock; tying the lighting system to the sim daylight value (W02-21) and the building / Worker Drone T1 animations (W02-18..20) are deferred.
    - **Playground:** open `<client>/playground.html?inspect=robot.hero&work=1&workTarget=tree&lights=1` → **see** the HQ bot in its work animation firing two hand lasers into a burst of tree chunks, and the **`LIGHTS`** panel (toolbar `Lights [L]`). Deposit stages: `<client>/playground.html?inspect=resource.metalDeposit&depositStage=half` (and `=depleted`).

## When you're done

- **All good:** from the main checkout run `npm run wave -- land 02`. It re-runs the gates, merges W02 into `main`, tags `wave-02`, pushes, and removes the W02 worktree and branch. (Plain `npm run wave` does the same and then opens the next ready wave.)
- **Something is wrong:** tell the wave lead which step and what you saw; it is fixed on the wave branch before anything lands.
