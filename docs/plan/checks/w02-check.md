# W02 — First Light: owner check

> The wave's demo story ([`waves/W02-first-light.md`](../waves/W02-first-light.md)) as a hands-on checklist. Format and rules: [`README.md`](./README.md). About 10 minutes.

## Before you start

- **Where it runs:** branch `wave/w02-first-light`, worktree `../TerraAgent-wt/w02`. Your main checkout is untouched.
- **Ports:** this worktree's `.env.worktree` sets `TA_PORT_BASE=4100` → app **4101**, client **5273** (`TA_DB_NAME=terra_wave_w02_first_light`). Below, `<client>` = `http://127.0.0.1:5273`, `<app>` = `http://127.0.0.1:4101`.
- **Start the stack** (if it isn't running): `cd ../TerraAgent-wt/w02 && npm run dev` (Docker must be running for Postgres). Stop it with Ctrl-C, or `kill $(cat .dev/runner.pid)` if it was started in the background.
- **Dev console:** in the game page press **`` ` ``** (the key under Esc). A dark panel opens at the bottom; type commands there and press Enter. It is **not** the browser's DevTools console. Click the game first if DevTools has focus.
- **Menus & queue shortcuts:** the menus are tabs of one Command Dock (bottom-left, `HIDE` / `SHOW`): press **`B`** (or click **`BUILD [B]`**) for the Construction menu, **`U`** (or click **`PRODUCTION [U]`**) for the Units menu, **`T`** (or click **`TERRAFORM [T]`**) for the Terraform tools, and click **`QUEUE`** for the Order Queue (**`Q`** collapses / expands it).
- Every tool used below is described in [`docs/dev/DEV_TOOLS.md`](../../dev/DEV_TOOLS.md).

## Steps

1. **Morning colony, family resource bar & power widget.** Open `<client>/play.html?scenario=w02` → a temperate hex planet loads in the morning (`06:00`) with a completed Command Hub T1 and HQ unit at `(0, 0)` surrounded by surface metals, minerals and forest deposits. At the top-centre **see** the family resource bar with three family chips — **Metals `400/500`**, **Minerals `250/500`**, **Bio `300/500`** (hover any chip to see its T1/T2/T3 sub-resource breakdown: `metals.t1 400`, `minerals.t1 250`, `bio.biomass 150`, `bio.water 150`) — plus the **Energy `+3 E/s`** chip. In the top bar, right after the energy chip, **see** the planet clock with a sun icon (`☀ 06:00` and the daylight %); hover the energy chip to see **`Income (Supply): +8 E/s`** / **`Spending (Demand): −5 E/s`**.
2. **Produce a Harvester from the Hub.** Press **`U`** (or click **`PRODUCTION [U]`**) to open the Units menu, select **Harvester** (`40 M · 15 Min · 5 Bio · 10 s`) and click **QUEUE** → **see** the Units lane entry in the Order Queue go **`IN PROGRESS`**, and stock drop gradually while it builds (paid as progress is made, `40/15/5/0` in total: `Metals 360`, `Minerals 235`, `Bio 295` when it is done). About 10 s later (or ~1 s with `warp 10` in the dev console) a Harvester rolls out from the Hub edge and starts harvesting the nearest surface deposit by itself (`+0.5/s`).
3. **Place a Metals Extractor and a Solar Collector.** Press **`B`** (or click **`BUILD [B]`**) to open the Construction menu:
   1. Select **Metals Extractor T1** (`65 M · 20 Min · 10 W · 14 s · −6 E/s`). Hover near the metals deposit at `(3, 0)` — off the deposit the ghost turns red (`must cover a metals deposit`); on `(3, 0)` the deposit tiles glow and the ghost shows the prep bill. Click to place.
   2. Select **Solar Collector T1** (`60 M · 75 Min · 10 Bio · 5 W · 18 s · +20 E/s`) and click a buildable 2×2 spot within 3 tiles of the Hub (e.g. `(-3, 2)`).
   3. **See** both orders appear in the Construction lane, run linked terrain prep and then construct one after the other as the HQ walks between them (type `warp 10` in the dev console to speed up construction, then `warp 1`).
4. **Extractor income and Solar power.** Once both buildings finish → **see** the Metals chip income rise by **`+1.0/s`** (× deposit richness) while powered, and the energy chip's hover show Solar contributing **`+20 E/s`** (`+28` supply total; type `power` in the dev console to print the component supply/demand breakdown).
5. **Queue waiting on missing resources + live ETA.** In the dev console run `fill minerals.t1 5` (sets `minerals.t1` to `5/500`, below the Extractor's `20 Min` cost), then place a second **Metals Extractor T1** on an uncovered metals deposit tile (e.g. `(4, 0)`) → **see** `waiting: Minerals 5/20 · ~30 s at +0.5/s` in the Order Queue panel. When Minerals reach `20` (from Harvester income, or immediately if you run `fill minerals.t1 250`), **see** the order start automatically.
6. **Night brownout and sunrise recovery.** In the dev console run `time night` → **see** Solar output drop to `0` (`−1 E/s` internal drain), demand exceed supply, a red **`BROWNOUT`** banner appear at the top right, and the Metals Extractor shed (`offline: brownout`, extraction paused). Run `time day` (or `skipnight`) → **see** daylight and `+20 E/s` Solar restored, the brownout banner clear, and the Extractor resume production.
7. **Storage cap halt and `waiting(storage)`.** In the dev console run `fill metals.t1` (or `fill metals.t1 9999`) → **see** the Metals chip at **`500/500`** (highlighted at cap) and the Metals Extractor pause extraction. In the dev console run `tune economy_t1_basic.solar.costMetals 600` and place a Solar Collector → **see** the queue entry show **`waiting(storage)`** with a greyed-out **"build a Supply Depot"** hint (the Supply Depot arrives in W03). Run `tune reset` afterwards.
8. **Cancel refunds (50 % of what was paid in progress vs free while waiting).** Place a **Solar Collector T1** so it enters **`IN PROGRESS`** (it pays `60 M / 75 Min / 10 Bio / 5 W` gradually as it builds), let it reach about half, then click its **`×` (Cancel)** button in the Order Queue → **see** **50 %** of what it has paid so far refunded in the stock chips (at half built: about `+15 M`, `+19 Min`). Lower Minerals with `fill minerals.t1 5`, place another Solar so it stays **`WAITING`**, and click **`×` (Cancel)** → **see** it cancelled with nothing charged.
9. **Continuous terraform, repair, resource find, 3D hand-locked lasers & object particles.** Press **`T`** (or click **`TERRAFORM [T]`** in the dock) → **see** three buttons top to bottom: **`TERRAFORM [G]`**, **`REPAIR [R]`** (`40%` of base cost for a full repair, deducted progressively), **`DISMANTLE [Z]`** (`50%` refund of spent resources; `[X]` only stops the selected unit). `G` / `R` / `Z` work only while this tab is open: on the Build tab they do nothing, and a `Resource find · 1% per hex · t1 50% / t2 35% / t3 15%` row in the reference table, which also shows Harvester `0.50/s` and Extractor `1.00 Metals/s`. Click **TERRAFORM**, then a hex on the edge of the forest east of the HQ → the status reads `Terraform ordered … continues hex by hex`, the tool disarms (the next click selects normally), no hex is highlighted, the status line then follows the chain (`TERRAFORM TREES (q,r) 18% · hex 2`), and the HQ drone walks over, clears it and then **keeps going on its own** into the trees / rocks inside the colony's territory **closest to the hex you clicked**, ring by ring around it (it never runs off along the forest while closer obstacles are left) (the `w02` scenario levels every slope and crater, so it has about 15 such hexes), one hex at a time (`warp 10` speeds it up), and stops when none is left. While working (even from 1–2 hexes away, `2m–4m`), **see** the HQ drone's dual cutter lasers originate strictly from its animated left and right hands (`toolOrigins.ts`, with glowing palm muzzle orbs) and strike the target tree/rock prop (`terrain.props.instancesForTile`), spawning a 360° radial splash of 3D trunk/foliage or boulder chunks around the object (`@terra/render/particles`) and an overhead blue 3D progress bar (`unit-health-bars`). Terraformed hexes look like plain flat grass (no metal plates); the rock ridge north of the HQ shows no dark quad, pale stripes or brown smear around it. Press **`X`** with the drone selected → it stops and the chain ends; then press **Esc**. Click **REPAIR** then empty ground → `Nothing to repair here`. In the dev console run `tune terraformFinds.deposit.chance 1`, order TERRAFORM again → each finished hex now finds a deposit: **see** a toast `Resource found: … (T<n>) deposit at (q, r)`, click the deposit → its card title ends in `· T<n>`; tier 2/3 clusters are bigger (4–5 nodes). `spawndeposit 2 2 metals 3` places one directly; `tune reset` restores the 1% chance. A Harvester on a T2/T3 metals deposit raises the `metals.t2` / `metals.t3` line in the Metals chip breakdown. The three tier looks side by side: `<client>/playground.html?inspect=robot.hero&biome=forestRock&view=iso&hexterrain=1`, zoom out, the row of three Metals clusters in front of the model (3 / 4 / 5 nodes).
10. **Bottom selection bar, finite 3-stage multi-harvester deposits, day/night light shutoff & Playground QA.**
    - **Selection & finite deposits:** Click any surface deposit → **see** the bottom selection bar show the deposit's resource family, visual stage (`FULL` $>50\%$, `HALF` $1\text{–}50\%$, or `DEPLETED` $0\%$ rubble), and `remaining / 500` reserves. Produce up to 6 Harvesters and right-click the same deposit → **see** up to 6 Harvesters park on the 6 adjacent hexes (`hexDistance === 1`, never clipping inside the deposit) and harvest simultaneously as the 3D mesh transitions from `full` to `half` to `depleted`.
    - **Day/night lights & moon shadows:** During daytime (`time 12:00`), **see** unit headlamps and building floodlights shut off (`daylight >= 0.65`). At night (`time 00:00`), **see** 4-sided building illumination and unit lights turn on, plus crisp cool-blue lunar shadows (press **`F8`** → **`SHADOW`** tab to inspect `Shadow Source Mode: Auto (Sun Day / Moon Night)` and `Night Moon Shadow`).
    - **Playground 3D Particles, Deposits & Lights Editor:** Open `<client>/playground.html?inspect=robot.hero&work=1&workTarget=tree&lights=1` → **see** the HQ bot in its full-channel `uWorkWeight` work animation firing dual hand-locked lasers into a 3D tree-felling particle fountain (`window.__pg.setWorkTarget('rock'|'dune'|'hole'|'flat'|'metals'|'minerals'|'oil'|'repair')`, `window.__pg.setDepositStage('full'|'half'|'depleted')`), and the interactive **`LIGHTS`** panel (`[L]`, `window.__pg.exportLightsCode()`).
