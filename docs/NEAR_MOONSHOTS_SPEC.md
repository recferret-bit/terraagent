### A.3 Live NEAR.AI Diplomatic Envoy Chat & Zero-Bluff TEE Deterrence

1. **Zero-Bluff Deterrence Proofs (Track `privacy`):**
   - Rival colonies submit fog-hidden army strength & stockpile snapshots into a **NEAR AI Cloud TEE Diplomatic Enclave**, which signs a verifiable attestation (`TEE ✓`): e.g., _"Colony Alpha's verified combat rating is `≥ 1.8x` Colony Beta"_—enabling credible deterrence **without exposing turret positions or unit composition**.
2. **Live Natural-Language Diplomatic Envoy Chat (Track `agents`):**
   - Click any neighbor colony on the minimap to negotiate in plain English with their **NEAR.AI Envoy** (_"Lower your Silica stall fee to 20 bp and join my defense against Crimson Guild for 4 NEAR"_).
   - **Anti-Spam Cost Rule (Caller Pays):** Sending an Envoy message consumes **`1` call from the sender's wallet `⚡ AI Compute Budget`** (`z-ai/glm-5.3-flash`), never draining the defender's budget!
   - **Prompt-Injection Immunity (Hard Deterministic Guardrail):** The Envoy LLM proposes a structured `NEP-413` + `bounty` counter-offer, which is validated **outside the LLM** by deterministic code (`colony-agent-governance.ts` VWAP price band + owner's signed reservation floor). Prompt injection can never trick an Envoy into signing a below-floor trade or unauthorized transfer.
