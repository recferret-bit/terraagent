TerraAgent · money in plain words · draft, 2026-11-02

Who earns what, and when

The project takes a small cut of every trade. Players earn by trading well, running a market stall and taking bounties. During the hackathon all of it is paid in cargo, which has no NEAR price yet.

The ownerEarns 0.25 % of every trade, plus premium items later

Pays everyone's gas and account costs. Needs many trades before the cut adds up.

The playersEarn stall fees, trading profit and bounty rewards

Real money only where NEAR or USDC is on the other side of a trade.

Today (Nov 2026)All earnings are in cargo, worth no NEAR

The fee mechanism is shown in the demo. NEAR-priced markets are proposed, not built.

01 · one trade

Where the money in one trade goes

A buyer pays 2 wNEAR (wrapped NEAR) for 1 000 water at a stall with a 1 % fee. Almost everything goes to the seller; the fee is split three ways.

02 · the owner

The owner: money in, money out

The rule from the master plan: the project earns from activity and never pays real NEAR out of a treasury. Costs start before income does.

03 · one planet, one week

How much one planet produces in fees

The balance model plays five colonies on one planet for a week. Fees are counted in cargo units. Turning them into NEAR needs a price for cargo, and nothing in the repo sets one.

04 · the players

The players: six ways to earn

The first three pay in cargo and work in the hackathon on the mock chain. The last three involve real NEAR and come later or are still proposed.

05 · what is needed

Tools needed

What has to exist for each money line to work.

For the owner

  • A fee account on NEARopen

    Receives the 0.25 % protocol leg. Not named yet (Q-03-7).

  • Fee legs in every trade batchmock on Nov 14

    The matcher adds stall, treasury and protocol legs to each NEAR Intents batch (W13, W22).

  • NEAR Intents with wNEAR and USDCproposed

    Needed before any fee or sale is worth real money. Mainnet only.

  • A funded relayer accountfrom Nov 10

    Pays gas and account storage for every player; alert below 5 NEAR.

  • Guild float accountsproposed

    5 NEAR per Guild trader, 10 NEAR in total; the most the Guild can lose.

  • Payment check for premium itemslater

    Accept NEAR or cargo for order slots, Guild visits and skins. No task exists yet.

  • Prize payout walletplanned · W25-31

    A mainnet account for the submission form, kept apart from the relayer and minter keys.

For the players

  • A Pilot IDfrom Nov 10

    Social login creates a NEAR account; no wallet or gas needed (NEAR Auth proposed, custodial fallback).

  • A Trade Hub in the colonymock on Nov 14

    The building that exports cargo and makes the colony a stall that earns fees.

  • Cargo on the colony accountfrom Nov 10

    NEP-245 tokens, created only by exporting stock through the Trade Hub.

  • A claimed account and a NEAR walletlater

    To hold NEAR, take it out of the game and stake it. A wallet through near-connect.

  • A staking poollater

    Any normal NEAR pool, used only after a colony is Settled.

  • For an AI agent: the Colony Agent APIproposed

    An agent plays a colony and earns the same ways, inside a limit the server enforces.

06 · what we promise and what we don't

Ground rules

No promise about the value of cargo or any token.

In the hackathon, every fee is paid in cargo and has no NEAR value. The README says so.

The planet treasury spends on in-game projects and never pays out NEAR.

Bots can farm cargo in the beta. That is an accepted risk (R02-35).

Open: check the law where players live before NEAR or USDC trading opens to the public. The pack does not cover this.
