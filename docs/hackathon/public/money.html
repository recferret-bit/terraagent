06 · what we promise and what we don't

Ground rules

No promise about the market price of cargo or any token; prices float freely by supply and demand.

Zero combat or mining Pay-to-Win: Day-1 staking unlocks Managed NEAR.AI Autopilot & 50% lower trade fees only; @terra/sim is 100% isolated from staking.

The Planet Council DAO spends its 20% fee slice on shared planetary projects (meteor_shield, terraform_boost, guild_subsidy) and never prints tokens.

AI agents cannot stake NEAR (403 AI_STAKING_FORBIDDEN), lock human NEAR in bonds, or break active truces/wars on a human's behalf (AW-13).

Jurisdictional compliance & KYC checks remain required before opening real-cash markets in restricted regions.
